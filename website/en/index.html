01

Log in seconds

Sets, reps and weight for every exercise. What you did last time is already filled in – adjust with −/+ 2.5 kg and tick it off.

02

Rest timer & Live Activity

The rest timer starts after every set. Your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show exercise, sets and rest – even with your phone in your pocket.

03

Watch yourself grow

Charts of your heaviest set, estimated max and volume. Slide your finger along the line for any workout, pick 1M to all time, with min, average and max.

04

Back in time

A calendar with a dot on every training day. Tap a date and see exactly what you did that day.

05

Your pass at the door

Your access QR code or barcode sits on Today, right under Start workout. Full screen, brightness up – on your Apple Watch too.

06

No more notes

Paste your old notes and Liftr turns them into workouts: “Leg press 3x12 40 kg” becomes a proper exercise with sets.
