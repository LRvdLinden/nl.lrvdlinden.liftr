Privacy

Liftr is built to keep your training private. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

What data

Liftr stores what you enter yourself: workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, routines, body measurements, notes and the code of your gym pass. There is no account and Liftr has no server of its own.

Storage and iCloud

Your data is stored on your device. With iCloud turned on, it syncs between your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch through your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot see this data. The code of your gym pass is stored end-to-end encrypted.

Apple Health

Only with your permission, Liftr reads your weight, body fat, lean body mass, height, heart rate and resting heart rate, and saves workouts, active energy, distance, weight and body fat to Apple Health. Health data is never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never leaves your devices through Liftr. You can change access at any time in the Health app.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are only used to read the QR code or barcode of your gym pass. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders and the rest timer are local notifications planned on your device. There is no push server.

No tracking

Liftr contains no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code and no tracking of any kind. This website uses no cookies and no trackers.

Deleting your data

Delete workouts in the app. To remove everything, delete Liftr from your devices and remove its data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Liftr.
